            Start a new race

            Start at lap 1, then press Start Race. A later entry keeps Leclerc on his starting tyre set with the corresponding simulated age. His historical stops are excluded. Existing Austin runs stay saved.

            You are the strategy director.

            1. Advance the race. Press Start Race for continuous playback; pause whenever you like. Go to Lap jumps directly and refreshes the three agents. Routine stay-out runs automatically. A change to strategy requires your approval.
            2. Read the call. Rival Analyst, Race Conditions & Incidents, and Leclerc Performance run their tools. The Gemini Director combines their reports and your saved intent.
            3. Make the call. Box now, stay out, or authorize a delayed stop. No historical stop or agent can box Leclerc for you.
            4. Try an incident. Inject a broken wing, puncture, rain or safety car. Watch the recommendation change, then approve a repair or continue with the damage.

            Rivals keep their historical pace and pit stops. Tyre wear, damage loss and pit costs are demo assumptions, not calibrated car physics. Future observations remain hidden until their replay time. Every skipped lap still processes your current tyres, damage and approved stops.
